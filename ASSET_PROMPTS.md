### Final prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: transparent PNG character sprite for a native iOS mood app. Edit target: the attached screenshot. Extract ONLY the complete happy fluffy cream robot plush avatar, preserving its original identity and high quality soft fur, black oval glass face, white curved closed happy eyes, tiny glowing ice blue open smile, raised grey mitten hands and grey soles, original cheerful pose and body proportions. ## Happy meadow

Saved as `MoodDay/Assets.xcassets/HappyMeadow.imageset/happy-meadow.png`.

### Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square scenery background plate for the happy mood in a premium native iPhone emotional wellbeing app. A dreamy, peaceful sunlit miniature meadow rendered as a tactile 3D storybook diorama. Straight-on low camera. Top 45% is almost empty warm ivory cream sky (#FFF8E8), blending seamlessly into that solid cream at the top edge. Below: layers of gently undulating sage-green and pale pistachio hills, soft velvety felt grass; softly sculpted round little trees far left and far right at the horizon. Tiny ivory daisies with golden centers and little butter-yellow wildflowers sparsely dotted on the foreground grassy slopes at bottom corners. A small curving warm cream footpath comes from the bottom center toward the distant middle. Keep central area simple and open, to later place a large avatar there. Beautiful soft ambient occlusion and shallow depth of field, warm morning sunlight, refined muted palette, airy editorial composition, exquisite tactile rendering. No sun, no clouds (these will be animated in code), no characters, no people, no animals, no words, no letters, no interface, no frame, no watermark. Do not put a large tree in center. Lighthearted, happy, hopeful, tender mood. Avoid bright saturated colors, photoreal outdoor photography, dark shadows, flat vector illustration.


## Avatar mouth color standard (all current and future moods)
Every avatar mouth must be soft warm white matching the cream fur highlights (target #FFF8EE), never cyan or blue. Preserve each mood's mouth shape and expression; avoid colored glow. Apply this rule to new moods, regenerated artwork, and animation assets. This supersedes any blue-mouth wording in earlier historical prompts.
